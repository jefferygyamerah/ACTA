## What must not be missed before delivery

1. The opening identifies the branch employee and document-review outcome.
2. The case and guide are visibly synthetic.
3. The local inference demonstration uses the actual QVAC SDK and a matching run receipt. GPU activity alone is insufficient attribution.
4. At least one source comparison/confirmation is visible. Model proposals are not presented as guaranteed correct.
5. First visit: two justified gaps; valid identification retained.
6. Second visit: explicit replacement and income evidence resolve the gaps in the same case.
7. Save/reload and two retained reviews are visible. Exact integrity checks are distinguished from bank identity or account approval.
8. One concise technical explanation distinguishes AI reading from deterministic comparison.
9. The result returns to the initial problem; future bank validation is framed as a next step.
10. The final video is Spanish, below five minutes, readable/audible, and accessible without credentials.
11. The repository remains accessible throughout judging, with preexisting bases declared in README. Keep the user's requested Vigía acknowledgement in repository documentation.
12. Both supplied rubrics are reconciled. Obtain user approval of the exact revised script and format before narration or final production.
