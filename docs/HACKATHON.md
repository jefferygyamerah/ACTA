Official event and track pages checked on **10 September 2026**. This is a preparation record, not a submission receipt or an eligibility decision.

## Event and deadline

- **Event:** [Decentralized AI Hackathon](https://www.trydojo.io/hackathons/decentralized-ai-hackathon), 9–11 September 2026, within ISD Summit; Tether is the technical partner.
- **Build window:** 9 September at 08:00 through 11 September at 08:00, Panama time. The 48-hour build is remote; attending the Panama City activities on 11 September is optional.

Sources: [official event](https://www.trydojo.io/hackathons/decentralized-ai-hackathon) and [published rules](https://www.trydojo.io/hackathons/decentralized-ai-hackathon?tab=rules). The Rules tab also links the full participation terms, which take precedence over the site's summary.

## ACTA's chosen challenge

**Track 05 — Caja de Ahorros: Soluciones de AI Descentralizada para la Banca.** The challenge offers **1,500 USDT** and accepts a range of banking applications; it does not mandate one workflow. Internal procedure assistance and document processing are among its suggested areas. [Official challenge brief](https://www.trydojo.io/hackathons/decentralized-ai-hackathon?tab=tracks).

ACTA proposes a branch-staff workflow: review a synthetic incomplete request, show the supporting procedure and version, record what is missing, and leave a clear handoff for the next colleague. A reviewed customer-contact template is an optional supporting step. The [product plan](plan.md) defines this scope; [Panama research](research-panama.md) distinguishes sector evidence from assumptions that still need validation inside a bank.

## Requirements applied to this entry

- Use QVAC for inference on the device or through peer-to-peer execution. Cloud inference is disqualifying. Non-inference hosting or authentication is permitted; Pears is optional.
- Use synthetic or public data. Real customer data from **any** financial institution is prohibited. ACTA adopts the narrower synthetic-only demonstration boundary in [privacy/security](privacy-security.md).
- Give judges repository access throughout evaluation. This track does not require an open-source license; ACTA's MIT license is its own licensing choice. The repository was private when checked, and access still needs verification.
- Supply a dedicated demonstration video of **at most five minutes**, narrated in **Spanish**, with a link that opens without credentials.
- Declare pre-existing foundations in the README. The general rules allow libraries, public templates, open source and AI coding assistants, but require substantial product work within the build window.

These requirements come from the [banking brief](https://www.trydojo.io/hackathons/decentralized-ai-hackathon?tab=tracks) and [general rules](https://www.trydojo.io/hackathons/decentralized-ai-hackathon?tab=rules).

## Evaluation and evidence

Caja de Ahorros selects its challenge winner using its own criteria after ISD checks the technical requirement. The brief emphasizes banking applicability, the practical benefit of local execution, and demonstration quality. The separate general ranking uses Technical 35%, Innovation 25%, Impact 20%, Design 10% and Completion 10%. [Evaluation sources](https://www.trydojo.io/hackathons/decentralized-ai-hackathon?tab=tracks).
