# ACTA decision record

Recorded 10 September 2026. These are product decisions and scope constraints, not implementation results. Later user decisions take precedence and should update the relevant canonical document.

| ID | Decision | Reason and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | Name the project ACTA. | User-selected name. “Sucursal” in earlier planning was a working title. The upstream directory `src/sucursal/` retains its original technical name. |
| D02 | Focus this submission on the banking challenge. | A teammate handles Vigía. ACTA gets its own banking narrative and a video of at most five minutes. |
| D03 | Optimize one incomplete-request review and handoff. | Sector research supports interpretation and coordination friction; Caja-specific unmet demand is still a hypothesis. |
| D04 | Start from the inspected Vigía banking module. | Reuse the relevant local inference, source-backed procedure selection and case-history patterns, preserving upstream attribution. Extraction has not happened in this repository. |
| D05 | Borrow Notare's review interaction; defer its clinical state and vault port. | Source visibility and deliberate review fit the task. Patient/encounter state is outside the banking workflow. |
| D06 | Keep customer-contact assistance as controlled templates. | Use confirmed missing-item categories and approved next steps. Names and contact details are unnecessary for composition. No sending capability or messaging credentials belong in this prototype. |
| D07 | Use synthetic data throughout this demonstration. | The inspected source records are plaintext. Local inference and signatures do not establish confidentiality or legal compliance. |
| D08 | Separate review closure, request disposition and actual contact. | A saved/closed review is not account approval, resolved documentation or proof of message delivery. |
| D09 | Keep source versions visible and abstain outside coverage. | The assistant must not invent bank procedures, deadlines, document-submission links or approval promises. |
| D10 | Measure demonstrated behavior before claiming impact. | Existing source results are historical, self-reported evidence. ACTA needs its own observed test runs and an appropriate baseline for time-saved claims. |
| D11 | Establish a private, documentation-first repository. | Public or judge access must be resolved before submission; no publication of real customer records is planned. |

## Time and prioritization

The user originally reported 18 hours remaining and one available builder. That was a planning constraint, not a timer reset. The nominal schedule in [the plan](plan.md) must be adjusted to actual remaining time.

The contact-template feature has a 60–90-minute maximum inside the case-interface block, replacing a new automatic checklist. Core startup, reliable case review, evaluation and the five-minute video take priority.

## Research interpretation

The initial broad “banking document assistant” positioning was narrowed after finding a close local vendor-reported deployment and existing Caja customer-assistance tools. The [research brief](research-panama.md) owns the evidence, source links and confidence limits.

Customer communication was retained only in the narrower form described in [privacy and security](privacy-security.md). That document owns the output boundaries and real-data pilot conditions.
